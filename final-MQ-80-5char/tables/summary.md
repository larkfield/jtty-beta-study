# Results by SNR

Trials are transmissions; units are what Decode % counts: 5-character groups of the sent text (a callsign's worth; a final 1-4 characters form a short group of their own) printed exactly, as one unbroken piece, for every mode (an FT8 or FT4 message gives all its groups or none). Decode intervals come from a bootstrap over whole trials, so groups sharing a transmission are not treated as independent; every Copy % interval is a Wilson interval over trials. A contest-macro series counts one unit per whole macro, copied only when every field printed exactly in some repetition and none printed a conflicting value (ambiguous).

Copy % is whole messages printed exactly. Garbage is wrong units printed per 1000 trials (upper 95% bound in brackets when none were seen). Chars/min is throughput: 60 x characters copied / seconds of airtime, both summed over the trials at that SNR. Characters are those of the normalized sent text (spaces between words count, framing does not). Copied: RTTY/PSK31 the characters of 5-character groups printed whole; JTTY the text of correctly decoded frames with each frame's edge spaces left out (so spaces at frame boundaries do not count); FT8/FT4 the whole message when decoded; contest macros the macro's words when the macro counts as copied. Airtime: the whole transmission (RTTY/PSK31 lead-in, idle, framing, envelope and tail included; JTTY whole 1.888 s frames), or a whole 15 s / 7.5 s T/R slot per FT8/FT4 message. True SNR is measured from each trial's samples; reported SNR is the decoders' mean report, from a censored-normal fit where reports hit the decoder's floor (count in *At floor*).
